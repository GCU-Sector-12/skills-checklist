How to submit

  1. Type your first and last name and fill in the checklist below.
  2. Click Save as CSV at the bottom. A file named after you (e.g. Jan_Kowalski.csv) is saved to your Downloads folder. Do not rename it.
  3. Open Microsoft Teams → go to the project team → Files tab.
  4. Click Upload → Files, pick your CSV file from Downloads and upload it. (You can also drag the file into the Files tab.)
  5. Done. To change your answers later: open this page, click Load my CSV (below), pick your file, update it, save again and upload – choose Replace when Teams asks.
